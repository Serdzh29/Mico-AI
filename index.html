Mico isn't just another chat window. It's a calm assistant that lives alongside your programs, understands what you're doing and helps without getting in the way.

All processing happens on your PC. Chat and screen models are downloaded once and then run locally. Your questions and messages don't go to the cloud unless it's necessary.

Mico isn't tied to one scenario. It can answer a question, explain an error on screen, play music, open the right Windows settings page, or just chat while you work.

Personality matters. Mico is a small peach-colored helper with a friendly face. It greets you by time of day, is happy when you come back, and doesn't feel like a boring tool.

It learns from what you do and gets more accurate suggestions over time. And when you need quiet, it can hide completely with one switch.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Mico works on Windows 10 and Windows 11.

Do I need internet?

Internet is needed to download models, for the weather and for voice components. Chat with installed models works locally.

Does my data go to the cloud?

Models run on your computer. Conversations and habit memory are stored locally, and you can clear them.

Why does Mico use so much memory?

Language models need RAM: the bigger the model, the more resources it needs. We're working on reducing the load.

How do I choose a model?

In the download list, models are split into chat models and screen models. If your computer isn't the most powerful, start with a compact one.

How do I uninstall Mico?

Uninstall Mico through Windows Settings, like any other app.

Privacy, license and security.

Last updated October 26, 2026. This explains how Mico handles your data, the terms of use, and how to protect your computer.

Privacy policy

What stays on your computer. Your conversations with Mico, voice requests and replies are processed by local models on your PC. Memory of actions and habits is stored on your device. You can view and clear it in settings.

Location. When Mico starts, it may ask for your location to show the weather. Coordinates are used only for the weather request. Declining location doesn't affect the other features.

Downloading models and components. To install and update Mico, it downloads models and voice components from download servers. Technical data needed for the download is transmitted at that time.

What we don't do. We don't sell your data and don't share it with third parties for advertising. Mico shows no ads.

Your rights. You can view and delete saved memory, turn off action memory, or uninstall Mico entirely at any time. Send data questions to: mico.ai@hotmail.com

License and terms of use

License. Mico is protected by copyright. You get the right to install and use it on your computers for personal purposes.

What's prohibited. You may not copy, modify, resell or pass it off as your own, or disassemble the program to create a similar product, unless a separate agreement allows it.

Third-party components. Mico uses third-party models and voice engines. Each has its own license, listed in the program folder.

Warranty. Mico is provided “as is”. We try to keep it stable, but we don't guarantee it will run without crashes on every hardware configuration.

Security

Download only from the official address. Don't get Mico from third-party sites or from messages sent by strangers.

Verify the file. A SHA-256 checksum is published next to the installer link. Compare it with the checksum of the file on your computer. If they don't match, don't run the installer.

Windows warning. SmartScreen may show a warning about an unknown publisher, especially for new programs. This doesn't mean the file is dangerous, but make sure you downloaded it from the official site.

Models. Download models only through Mico. Don't add models from unknown sources.

Updates. Keep Windows and Mico up to date. Security fixes are released regularly.

Report a vulnerability. If you find a security bug, email us at: mico.ai@hotmail.com. We'll respond and fix it.

*

Mico is completely free for now, and all of its features are available to everyone. We don't rule out paid features and/or subscriptions in the future.
